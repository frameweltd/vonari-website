Canada

Canada continues to be one of the most welcoming destinations for international students, with clear pathways from study permit to permanent residency for those who choose to stay.

  • Study Permits processed with complete documentation support
  • Co-op programs that combine study with paid work experience
  • PGWP (Post-Graduation Work Permit) pathway guidance
  • Partner colleges and universities across Ontario, BC, and beyond
