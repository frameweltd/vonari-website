Everything you need, in one place.

SERVICE 01

University Selection

Choosing where to study is one of the most important decisions of your life — we make sure it's an informed one. We assess your academic record, budget, and career goals to shortlist universities and colleges in the USA and Canada that are the right fit, not just the most famous name.

  • Personalized shortlist of accredited institutions
  • Program and course matching to your career goals
  • Application document preparation and review
  • Admission letter follow-up and guidance
SERVICE 02

Visa Application

Visa processes are detail-heavy and unforgiving of small mistakes. We help you assemble a complete, accurate application and prepare you for what comes next — including the interview, which is often the part applicants worry about most.

  • Document checklist and verification
  • Visa interview coaching and mock sessions
  • Application form review before submission
  • Updates at every stage of processing
SERVICE 03

Recruitment & Placement

For professionals and institutions alike, we bridge the gap between qualified candidates and opportunities abroad. Whether you're seeking a diploma placement or an employer is seeking international talent, we manage the matching process end-to-end.

  • Candidate-to-institution matching
  • Employer and program liaison
  • Placement confirmation support
  • Pre-departure orientation
SERVICE 04

Scholarship Guidance

Funding shouldn't be the reason a dream stays on hold. We help you identify scholarships and financial aid programs you genuinely qualify for, and guide you through crafting a compelling, honest application.

  • Scholarship and financial aid research
  • Eligibility assessment
  • Personal statement and essay guidance
  • Application deadline tracking
SERVICE 05

Airport Pick Up

Arriving in a new country for the first time is a big moment — we make sure you're not navigating it alone. We coordinate airport pick up so you have a familiar contact and a smooth start to life abroad.

  • Arrival coordination and scheduling
  • Airport pick up on your travel date
  • Orientation on first-week essentials
  • Ongoing local contact information
