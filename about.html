Our Story

Why we started Vonari

Vonari Educational Agency began in Kabarnet with a simple observation: talented students and professionals across Baringo County and beyond had the drive to study and work abroad, but not always the guidance to navigate the process.

University applications, visa paperwork, and scholarship searches can be overwhelming when you're doing it for the first time — and doing it alone. We built Vonari to be the steady hand through that process: a local office you can walk into, and a team that stays with you from your first consultation to the day you land in your new city.

Today, we've guided students into universities and workplaces across the United States and Canada, and we're continuing to grow our reach to more destinations.

Our Mission

Guidance. Transparency. Care.

  • Guidance — We don't just process paperwork. We help you choose the right path, the right school, and the right country for your goals.
  • Transparency — You will always know where your application stands. No jargon, no vague timelines, no surprises.
  • Care — Every student who walks through our door in Kabarnet is treated as a person with a future to build, not a file number.
